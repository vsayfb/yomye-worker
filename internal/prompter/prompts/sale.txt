Extract structured sale details from the Turkish marketplace text.

Return valid JSON only:
{
  "category": "",
  "title": "",
  "price_amount": null,
  "price_currency": null,
  "pricing_type": "contact",
  "condition": null,
  "brand": null,
  "model": null,
  "expires_at": null
}

Rules:
- category is the primary item type in normalized, singular Turkish.
- title is a short natural Turkish marketplace title, maximum 80 characters.
- pricing_type is fixed, negotiable, free, or contact.
- fixed and negotiable require a positive price and ISO-4217 currency.
- use TRY for an amount expressed as TL.
- free and contact require null price_amount and price_currency.
- condition is null, new, like_new, good, fair, or poor.
- expires_at is always null.
- Do not invent missing details.
