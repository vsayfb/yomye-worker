Extract structured gig details from marketplace text written in any language.

The provider may be an individual, tradesperson, team, shop, or small business. Preserve the
service concept without turning every service into a person-role title.

Return valid JSON only:
{
  "title": "",
  "profession": "",
  "duration_type": "",
  "pay_amount": null,
  "pay_currency": "TRY",
  "expires_at": null
}

Rules:
- profession is the recognized reusable service, trade, or occupation.
- When the input names a service or business activity, use its natural provider-neutral name.
  Example: "Oto Yıkama" stays "Oto Yıkama"; do not invent "Detaylı Temizlik" or append "Uzmanı".
- Do not add adjacent services, specializations, or qualifications absent from the input.
- title is a short natural marketplace title in the source text's language, maximum 80 characters.
- duration_type is ONE_TIME, HOURLY, DAILY, WEEKLY, MONTHLY, or an empty string.
- pay_amount is numeric or null.
- pay_currency is an ISO-4217 code; use TRY when omitted.
- expires_at is always null.
- Do not invent missing details.
